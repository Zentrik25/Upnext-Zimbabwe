# Career Insights: twelve-article editorial plan — DRAFT (D-78)

Status: **DRAFT**. These are briefs, not articles. Nothing here is published, and nothing may be published until a
writer drafts it in the CMS and a different editor approves it against the ten-item checklist (D-78). Facts named
below are what the writer must **check against the source**, not claims made by this plan; where a figure or rule
changes (tax bands, minimum wages, programme dates), the article must state the date and the source it relied on.

Earlier plan: `CAREER_INSIGHTS_LAUNCH_PLAN.md` (twelve topics, 2026-11-14) and six drafts in `drafts/`. This plan
follows the owner's twelve topics of the CMS brief; overlaps are marked so no subject is written twice.

Common rules for every brief:

- **Audience**: Zimbabwean job seekers, mostly on phones, many applying for their first or second job.
- **Unique value**: practical Zimbabwe-specific steps tied to real UPNEXT listings (link the matching category list,
  not invented vacancies), plus what UPNEXT editors check. No generic advice that could be about any country.
- **Sources**: primary sources first (laws, regulators, the issuing organisation); news only for context and dated.
  List every source in the article's Sources and References with the date it was accessed.
- **Length**: what the subject needs, typically 900–1,600 words; no padding for search engines.
- **Links**: at least one relevant opportunity list and one related guide or article.
- **Pictures**: only pictures UPNEXT has the rights to, with alt text and credit; no stock-photo people presented as
  real candidates.

## Briefs

### 1. Writing a CV Zimbabwean employers can screen quickly
- **Reader question**: what should my CV contain, in what order, and how long should it be?
- **Research**: what UPNEXT listings actually ask applicants to send (count CV, certified copies, references, cover
  letter across current listings); professional-body registration where adverts require it.
- **Sources to consult**: current UPNEXT listings (counted, dated); professional bodies named in those adverts.
- **Must not**: promise a template "gets interviews".

### 2. Preparing for a job interview in Zimbabwe
- **Reader question**: what happens in panel, competency and practical interviews, and how do I prepare?
- **Research**: interview formats named in public-service and NGO recruitment notices; documents candidates are asked
  to bring.
- **Sources**: Public Service Commission recruitment notices; employers' own published recruitment pages.
- **Must not**: invent interview questions as if from a named employer.

### 3. Industrial attachment: finding a placement and making it count
- **Reader question**: when and how do I find an attachment, and what will the institution need from me?
- **Research**: institutions' attachment requirements (letters, logbooks, assessment), typical timing.
- **Sources**: university and polytechnic attachment offices' published guidance; UPNEXT internship listings.

### 4. Graduate trainee programmes: reading the rules and applying well
- **Reader question**: what do age, degree class and graduation-year rules mean, and how do I meet the deadline?
- **Research**: eligibility wording across current and recent programmes (dated); assessment stages.
- **Sources**: the programmes' own advertisements and pages.

### 5. Understanding PAYE on your payslip
- **Reader question**: why is PAYE deducted, how is it worked out, and what else appears on a payslip?
- **Research**: current tax tables and the currency they apply to; other statutory deductions (for example NSSA)
  and their current rates; the effective date of each.
- **Sources**: ZIMRA published tax tables and guidance; NSSA; the Finance Act in force. State the tax year.
- **Must not**: give personal tax advice; every figure dated and sourced, and re-checked before publication.

### 6. Employment contracts: what to check before you sign
- **Reader question**: what should a contract say, and what are fixed-term, permanent and probation arrangements?
- **Research**: the relevant provisions of the Labour Act [Chapter 28:01] as amended, and the amendment date.
- **Sources**: the Labour Act and amendments (official text); Ministry of Public Service, Labour and Social Welfare.
- **Must not**: give legal advice; point readers to the Ministry or a lawyer for their own case.

### 7. Spotting fake job adverts and recruitment scams
- Write only if it adds what the existing guide does not (for example new patterns seen in reports to UPNEXT,
  described without naming private individuals). Otherwise update the guide through its own review.
- **Sources**: Zimbabwe Republic Police public warnings; UPNEXT reporting data (counts only, dated).

### 8. Writing a cover letter that gets read
- **Reader question**: when is a cover letter needed and what goes in it?
- **Research**: how many current listings ask for one; how adverts phrase the request.
- **Sources**: UPNEXT listings (counted, dated).

### 9. Finding entry-level jobs with little experience
- **Reader question**: where are entry-level roles advertised and how do I compete without experience?
- **Research**: entry-level share of UPNEXT listings by category (counted, dated).
- **Sources**: UPNEXT listings; employers' published graduate and trainee pages.

### 10. Skills Zimbabwean employers ask for, and how to show them
- **Reader question**: which skills appear most in adverts, and how do I evidence them?
- **Research**: a dated count of skills named in UPNEXT listings across categories; method stated in the article.
- **Sources**: UPNEXT listings (method and date); recognised certification bodies where named.
- **Must not**: present the count as a national survey.

### 11. Judging a scholarship offer before you apply
- **Reader question**: is this scholarship genuine, and what does it really cover?
- **Research**: how real scholarships publish eligibility, funding and deadlines; warning signs (fees to apply).
- **Sources**: the funders' official pages; Ministry of Higher and Tertiary Education notices where relevant.

### 12. Planning your next career step
- **Reader question**: how do I move up or across, and what training or registration helps?
- **Research**: progression requirements stated in senior-role adverts (experience years, registration).
- **Sources**: UPNEXT listings (dated); professional bodies' published requirements.

## Sequence

Write and review in pairs so each review is independent: 5 and 6 first (highest reader need, strictest sourcing),
then 1, 2, 8 (applications), then 3, 4, 9, 11 (students and graduates), then 10 and 12. Topic 7 waits for a decision
on whether to update the existing guide instead.
