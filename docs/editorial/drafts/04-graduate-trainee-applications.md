## Read the eligibility rules the way a screener does

Programmes advertised in Zimbabwe in late 2026 showed the same kinds of rule:

| Rule seen in notices | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| "Graduates aged 26 or below" | An age limit, usually counted at the closing date. Over it, do not apply. |
| "Upper second class (2.1) or better" | The class must be on your CV and transcript. A 2.2 does not qualify. |
| "Graduated in 2025 or completing in 2026" | A graduation window; check the year on your certificate. |
| "Nine disciplines: mining, geology, surveying …" | Each discipline may have its own application link; apply to the one that matches your degree. |
| "24-month programme with rotations" | A fixed term. A permanent post afterwards is not promised unless stated. |
| "Certified copies will be verified" | Bring or send certified copies; originals may be checked at interview. |

If you do not meet a rule, do not apply hoping it will be overlooked; a large programme screens automatically.

## Certified copies

Employers often ask for "certified copies" of certificates and transcripts. A certified copy is a photocopy signed and
stamped as a true copy by an authorised person. [VERIFY: who may certify in Zimbabwe (for example a Commissioner of
Oaths, a legal practitioner or a police officer), whether certification expires for employers' purposes, and whether
a fee is normally charged.] Certify more copies than one application needs; you will reuse them.

## Several links, one programme

Some programmes publish one link per discipline, sometimes through a link shortener. Before you fill in a form, check
the page it opens is on the employer's own address or its recruitment system, and that the discipline matches. Never
pay to submit a form.

## A two-week plan for a programme you meet

1. Day 1: read the notice twice; list every requirement and document.
2. Days 2–4: certify copies; ask two referees; collect your transcript.
3. Days 5–8: write the CV and the motivation for this programme (see the CV guide).
4. Days 9–10: practise aptitude tests if the programme uses them. [VERIFY: whether editors want to point to free
   practice material.]
5. At least two days before the closing date: submit, keep the confirmation, note the reference.

## After you apply

Many notices say "only shortlisted candidates will be contacted". If you have heard nothing a few weeks after the
closing date, assume you were not shortlisted and keep applying. A genuine programme never asks you to pay for a
test, a medical or training.
