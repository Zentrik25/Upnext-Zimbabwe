Job notices in Zimbabwe use a familiar set of phrases. Knowing exactly what each one asks for helps you decide
quickly whether to apply and what to prepare.

## Phrases you will see, and what they mean

| Phrase | What it usually means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "5 O Levels including English and Mathematics" | Five passes at O Level, two of them in these subjects. [VERIFY: the pass grade employers mean, usually C or better.] | List subjects and grades on your CV. |
| "Certified copies of academic and professional qualifications" | Copies signed and stamped as true copies (see the graduate guide). | Send copies, never originals. |
| "Contactable referees" | People the employer can phone or e-mail now. | Give current numbers; ask first. |
| "Internal candidates" | Only current employees may apply. | Do not apply from outside the organisation. |
| "Contract" / "fixed term" | Employment for a stated period. | Note the length; ask about renewal at interview. |
| "Police clearance" | A certificate of your criminal record status. [VERIFY: where it is issued and how long it takes.] | Apply early; it can take time. |
| "Only shortlisted candidates will be contacted" | You will hear nothing if unsuccessful. | Keep a record of the date you applied. |
| "Single PDF" | All documents combined in one file, in the order asked. | Merge before sending. |
| "Hand delivered" / postal address | Paper application to a physical address. | Use the address exactly; keep a copy. |
| "Competitive salary" | Pay is not stated. | Ask only at offer or interview stage. |

## Closing dates

A deadline given as a date usually means the end of that day in Zimbabwe (Central Africa Time). On UPNEXT a listing
is marked closed when its deadline passes. Aim to apply at least two days early: e-mail systems and offices close,
and late applications are rarely considered.

## When the listing is unclear

If a notice does not say how to apply, gives conflicting dates, or names no organisation, do not guess. Check the
original notice on the organisation's own channel (see **How to verify a recruitment advertisement**) or report the
listing so editors can correct it.
