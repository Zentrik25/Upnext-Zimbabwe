## How attachment usually works in Zimbabwe

Most engineering degree and diploma courses at Zimbabwean universities and polytechnics include a period of
industrial attachment, during which you work at a company and are assessed by both the company and your institution.
[VERIFY: typical length by institution type, for example around a year for many degree programmes and a shorter
period for some diploma courses; confirm with two or three institution handbooks before stating figures.]

What this means in practice:

- Your institution normally issues an **attachment letter** and an assessment or logbook. Employers often ask for the
  letter before they consider you; get it early.
- Many placements are agreed months ahead. Start approaching employers at least a semester before your attachment
  period begins.
- An attachment may be paid an allowance or unpaid. Ask what is offered; a genuine placement never charges you.

## Where engineering placements come from

Look at the sectors that employ engineers in your discipline:

| Discipline | Kinds of employer to approach |
|---|---|
| Electrical | Power utilities and generation companies, mines, manufacturers, building services contractors |
| Civil | Rural district councils and municipalities, road and building contractors, consulting engineers |
| Mechanical | Manufacturers, mines, sugar and agro-processing, transport fleets and workshops |
| Mining and metallurgy | Gold, platinum, lithium and chrome mines, assay laboratories |
| Chemical | Food and beverage processing, fertiliser and chemicals producers, water treatment |

Councils and utilities do advertise graduate and trainee engineering posts on UPNEXT (for example a civil engineering
graduate trainee post at a rural district council in November 2026); students can use those notices to see which
organisations take engineers and to approach them about attachment.

## Professional registration starts now

Engineering practice in Zimbabwe is regulated by the Engineering Council of Zimbabwe, and the Zimbabwe Institution of
Engineers is the professional body. [VERIFY: the governing Act and its chapter number; student or graduate membership
categories and whether attachment experience counts towards later registration.] Keep a simple record of what you do
on attachment (projects, tasks, supervisor) from the first week; it is useful for your report and later for
registration.

## A short letter that works

```
Subject: Industrial attachment request – BEng Electrical Engineering, 3rd year

Dear [Name / The Engineering Manager],

I am a third-year Electrical Engineering student at [Institution] and must complete [length] of industrial
attachment from [month year]. I would like to work in your [maintenance / design / projects] section. I have used
[tools] in coursework and completed a project on [topic].

My CV and the institution's attachment letter are attached. I am available for an interview at any time and can be
reached on [phone].

Yours sincerely,
[Name]
```

Follow up once, politely, after one or two weeks.
