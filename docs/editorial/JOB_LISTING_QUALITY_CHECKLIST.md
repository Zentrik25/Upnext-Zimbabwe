# Job listing quality checklist — DRAFT

Status: DRAFT (D-74) for the owner's and editors' approval. It applies before a listing is submitted for review and
again when an editor reviews it. It does not change the workflow (D-18, D-22, D-46); it states what "complete and
credible" means so the second person has something concrete to check against. Nothing here may be met by inventing a
fact: when the original notice does not say something, the field stays empty (D-71 (5)) and, where useful, the
listing says the issuer did not state it.

## A. Before entering the listing

- [ ] **The original notice is in hand** (the employer's page, portal, PDF, newspaper scan or e-mail), not a repost.
      A WhatsApp image, a Facebook share or a job board copy is a lead, not a source: find the original.
- [ ] **The issuer is named and real.** The organisation can be found on its own website or official channel. An
      anonymous ("Confidential", "A leading organisation") notice is published only when a recruitment agency that
      is itself named and findable is the issuer, and then the agency is the organisation.
- [ ] **The notice is open to the public.** Internal-candidates-only adverts are not published.
- [ ] **It is still open** on the day of entry.

## B. Facts (from the notice only)

- [ ] Title exactly as advertised, with balanced brackets and no added words ("URGENT", "Apply now").
- [ ] Organisation: the legal or trading name the notice uses, consistent across title, summary and text.
- [ ] Place of work written as the notice gives it **and** a matching structured place selected, so the listing
      appears under locations and can carry JobPosting (the editor matches "Harare", "Kariba" etc. automatically
      once places exist; check the match).
- [ ] Employment type and work arrangement when stated.
- [ ] Closing date entered as a date; the same date in the instructions if repeated there. If the notice gives no
      date, leave it empty — never guess.
- [ ] Key responsibilities and qualifications as the notice lists them, in the listing's own sections, not pasted
      into the summary. If the notice is genuinely short, keep it short; do not pad it.
- [ ] Reference number when given.

## C. Application route

- [ ] One clear route: the employer's own portal (https), an e-mail address, or postal / hand-delivery instructions.
- [ ] **Never** a job board, aggregator or link shortener as the destination. Replace `lnkd.in`, `bit.ly` and similar
      with the address they open, after checking it is the employer's own.
- [ ] The e-mail address is the one in the original notice. A free webmail address (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook) is allowed
      when the notice uses it; the page then shows a note asking the reader to confirm the vacancy (D-74).
- [ ] WhatsApp-only routes need confirmation with the employer by another channel before publication.
- [ ] Instructions keep the employer's requirements (documents, subject line, format) and drop promotional text
      copied from elsewhere ("Share this opportunity …", "Follow our page …").

## D. Source and verification

- [ ] **A source is recorded for every listing**: the original notice's address (or a citation for print), with the
      date it was seen. The job page now shows it (D-74).
- [ ] The listing is marked **Verified** only when the agreed verification standard is met (owner decision O-3; until
      then, at minimum: the vacancy was found on the employer's own channel or confirmed with the employer). Anything
      else stays Not verified.
- [ ] The operator's own vacancies (Zentrik Solutions) say so (owner decision O-6).

## E. UPNEXT's own value

- [ ] The summary is written by UPNEXT in one or two plain sentences: what the role is, where, for whom. It is not
      the first lines of the advert.
- [ ] Category, sectors, experience level and skills are set, so the listing appears in the right lists and the
      related listings make sense.
- [ ] Featured images only when the employer supplied them or they are UPNEXT's own (D-58).

## F. After publication

- [ ] Reports on the listing are handled (D-41); corrections go through an amendment and a second review.
- [ ] A listing whose employer withdraws the post is withdrawn on UPNEXT the same day.
- [ ] Closing dates are checked weekly for listings without one; ask the employer or close the listing after a
      stated period (owner to set, for example 30 days).
