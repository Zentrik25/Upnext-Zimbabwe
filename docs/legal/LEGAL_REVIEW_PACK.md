# Legal review pack — DRAFT

Status: DRAFT (Phase 5, WS2). A starting package for the owner's lawyer. **It contains no legal conclusions, no assertion that the service complies with any law, and no approval.** The legal framework for Zimbabwe has not been researched by the engineering side; every question below is for the lawyer to answer.

## 1. What to review
1. The public drafts: `/privacy` and `/terms` (bracketed text marks every fact the owner or lawyer must supply).
2. DATA_INVENTORY.md: every kind of personal data, why, who sees it, retention, deletion.
3. RIGHTS_PROCEDURES.md: how requests are handled today.
4. BACKUP_AND_RESTORE.md (retention in backups) and PRODUCTION_CONFIGURATION.md (security measures).
5. EDITORIAL_HANDBOOK.md (what is published about organisations and contacts, takedown and corrections).

## 2. Questions for the lawyer
| # | Question |
|---|---|
| L-1 | Which Zimbabwean data-protection and cyber-related legislation applies to an operator of this service, and does it require registration, licensing or a designated officer? |
| L-2 | Who is the correct legal operator (the entity named in the notice), and what contact details must the notice show? |
| L-3 | What lawful bases support each purpose in DATA_INVENTORY.md (account, security records, e-mails the service needs)? Is consent needed for anything now built? |
| L-4 | Retention: acceptable periods for audit records, security records, backups; is the 14-day deletion cooling-off acceptable; must deletion be immediate on request? |
| L-5 | Rights: which rights must the notice state (access, correction, deletion, objection, portability), response times, the complaint route and regulator? Is a self-service export required, or is a manual process acceptable? |
| L-6 | Cross-border transfer: the e-mail provider, off-server backup and any monitoring service may be outside Zimbabwe. What conditions apply, and what must the notice say? |
| L-7 | Minimum age for accounts and for use of the service. |
| L-8 | Breach notification: duties, deadlines, who is told (a procedure is needed even though none is written yet). |
| L-9 | Terms: limits of liability for third-party listings, governing law and courts, intellectual property in summaries of public notices, rules for linking and quoting sources, takedown and complaint handling. |
| L-10 | Do the editorial rules on scam screening and takedown create duties or risks (defamation of issuers, false listings)? |
| L-11 | Whether staff accounts (editors) need a separate notice or contract terms about the audit trail kept about their actions. |
| L-12 | Does publishing business contact details taken from public notices raise any issue, and what correction route must exist? |

## 3. What the owner must supply first
Operator name, legal form and registered address; the contact address (`UPNEXT_CONTACT_EMAIL`); names of the providers once chosen (e-mail, hosting, backup, monitoring); the backup retention decision; who answers rights requests and within what time.

## 4. After the review
The owner records the outcome and the review date in the decision register, the bracketed text is replaced, the draft banner is removed and the pages become indexable. Until then they stay drafts and noindex.
