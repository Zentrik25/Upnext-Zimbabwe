## 2. Confirmed from the sources available (still subject to checking against the primary text)

1. The data protection law is the Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07]; **POTRAZ** is the designated data protection authority (S1, S3, S6).
2. Data subjects have at least rights of access, correction and deletion; a guardian may exercise a child's rights (S6, from the s.14 summary).
3. Transfers outside Zimbabwe are restricted to destinations ensuring an adequate level of protection, with further routes for others (ss.28 and 29) (S1, S6).
4. S.I. 155 of 2024 requires controllers to be **licensed** with POTRAZ and to **appoint a Data Protection Officer** and notify POTRAZ (reported form DP2); licences run for 12 months; tiers are set by the number of data subjects (Tier 1: 50 to 1,000; Tier 2: 1,001 to 100,000; Tier 3: 100,001 to 500,000; Tier 4: over 500,000) (S2 to S5). Sources disagree on compliance deadlines (12 September 2024 start; 12 December 2024 for DPO notification in one source; 12 March 2025 for licences in several) and on fees.
5. The Act's text, per one summary, requires notification of a security breach to the Authority within 24 hours (S6); whether and when affected people must be told is **unresolved** in the sources.
6. Online suppliers under the Consumer Protection Act must disclose their legal status and physical address to consumers (S7).
