# Decision register — SOURCE OF TRUTH
## CONFIRMED
- UPNEXT, Zimbabwe-first, categories: jobs, internships, graduate trainees, scholarships, tenders, grants.
- Approved flat/minimalist visual direction and HTML reference.
- Public browsing; accounts for personalisation and saved listings.
- Next.js/TypeScript/Tailwind; Django/DRF; PostgreSQL; Docker Compose.
- Modular monolith with extensible versioned APIs.
- Same VPS as The Granite Post; Traefik in front of Nginx; no disruption to existing service.
- Documentation and explicit approval before coding.

## OPEN — require owner decisions
- Domain and brand copy; editorial publishing policy (admin only vs employer submissions).
- Email verification and notification timing/provider.
- Exact application handoff (external-only for launch proposed, not confirmed).
- Detailed opportunity category fields, source verification policy, duplication rules.
- Privacy policy, retention and deletion process; legal review.
- Actual VPS capacity, routing configuration and deployment path.
- Quantified product KPIs, performance SLOs and accessibility audit process.

## Change control
Any change to CONFIRMED decisions requires a dated ADR with rationale, impact and explicit approval. Claude Code may propose but not silently approve changes.
