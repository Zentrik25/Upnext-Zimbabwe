# Product vision — DRAFT
**Product:** UPNEXT. **Market:** Zimbabwe first.

## Problem
Opportunity seekers navigate fragmented channels, inconsistent descriptions, unreliable deadlines and duplicate listings. Employers and institutions lack a consistent way to reach relevant candidates.

## Promise
One reliable destination to discover jobs, internships, graduate trainee programmes, scholarships, tenders and grants, with transparent sourcing and optional account-based personalisation.

## Principles
- Browsing and opportunity detail pages remain public.
- User accounts unlock profile-based recommendations, saved listings and preferences.
- Recommendations are relevance estimates, not eligibility guarantees.
- Every opportunity exposes provenance, application destination and verification state.
- Accessible, fast, flat and mobile-first interfaces.
- Respect privacy; minimise collected personal information.

## Success measures — targets TBD
Search-to-detail engagement, source-link click-through, saved opportunities, verified-listing coverage, expired-listing accuracy, repeat usage, account onboarding completion, accessibility and performance. Set baselines and numerical targets after instrumentation design.

## Non-goals at first release
UPNEXT is not an application-processing service, employer ATS, AI eligibility adjudicator or payment processor.
